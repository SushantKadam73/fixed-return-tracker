# data/

Committed datasets. Every record carries its source URL and dates.

- `banks/` — bank master list and lineage (merged predecessors).
- `sources/` — registry of the official pages and files the collectors read.
- `history/` — reconstructed historical rate cards and RBI-prescribed era rates.
- `schemes/` — Government scheme rate histories (PPF, SSY, EPF...).
- `series/` — macro series (CPI, CPI-IW, policy rates, bond yields, gold price...).
- `snapshots/` — latest summaries written by the collectors; the website falls back to these when Convex is unavailable.

Raw copies of bank web pages are never committed here.
