    Overview

    TMB Basic Recurring Deposit Account offers a simple approach to saving, making it easy for customers to set aside a fixed amount each month. This systematic saving method helps customers plan for future expenses

     
    Product Highlights

    This recurring deposit account provides stable and predictable growth through fixed monthly contributions. Customers benefit from attractive interest rates, guaranteed maturity value, and the ability to choose a tenure that fits their financial schedule

     
    Features and Benefits

    TMB Basic Recurring Deposit Account offers tenures ranging from short to long durations, enabling customers to plan savings easily. Monthly instalments remain fixed, ensuring consistency and helping develop disciplined saving habits

     
    Eligibility

    This account is available to all resident individuals, including minors under guardian operation. Customers must hold a TMB Savings Account or Current Account to enable monthly instalments

    Operational guidelines

    Simple rules to help you manage your Recurring Deposit smoothly

    Operational Guidelines

    . Depositors are required to pay each monthly instalment within 30 days from the due date. If the instalment is delayed beyond this period, penal interest will be charged at the rate applicable from time to time
    . Depositors have the flexibility to pay instalments in advance for any number of months, as per their convenience
    . For the purpose of calculating penal interest, any fraction of a month will be treated as a full month
    . Customers may opt for Standing Instructions (SI) to automatically transfer monthly instalments from their Savings Bank or Current Account, ensuring timely payments
    . If a depositor is habitually late in paying instalments, or if the account remains in arrears for a period exceeding three months, the Bank reserves the right to close the RD account at its discretion
    . In case of such closure, the depositor will be entitled to the amount already deposited along with interest applicable for the period the deposit remained with the Bank, after deducting a penalty of 1%
