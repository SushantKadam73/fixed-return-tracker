    Overview

    TMB Fixed Deposit Account offers a dependable and predictable way to grow your savings. Customers can choose from a range of tenures, making it easy to save for short-term or long-term goals

     
    Product Highlights

    This fixed deposit plan provides steady returns with flexible duration options and competitive interest rates. Customers benefit from assured maturity values, easy account management, and the freedom to select an interest payout method that suits their needs

     
    Features and Benefits

    TMB Fixed Deposit Account comes with a wide range of tenure options, nomination facilities, and transparent interest calculations. Customers can choose interest payout at maturity or at regular intervals as per eligibility

     
    Eligibility

    This deposit is available to all resident individuals, including minors under guardian operation, as well as joint account holders. Customers must complete standard KYC documentation at any TMB branch

    Operational guidelines

    Simple rules to help you manage your Fixed Deposit smoothly

    Period of Deposits / Highlights

    . Deposits can be made for periods ranging from 7 days to 120 months
    . Fixed deposits accepted for a defined tenure
    . Simple interest option available for periodic payouts
    . Nomination facility available
    . Premature closure permitted, subject to applicable penalty
    . Loan/Overdraft facility available against deposit value
    . Flexible interest payout options – Monthly or Quarterly
    . Standing Instructions (SI) accepted for automatic interest credit

    As per the section 206AA of the Income Tax Act, quoting of Permanent Account Number (PAN) is mandatory, if the interest on term deposits for the financial year exceeds Rs. 50,000.00 for ordinary citizens and Rs. 1,00,000.00 for senior citizens. If PAN is not furnished, then tax has to be deducted at higher rate of 20%. Quoting of PAN in the declaration form 121 is mandatory for non-deduction of tax at source. If PAN is not quoted in the form 121, tax will be deducted at a higher rate of 20%.

    Therefore, the customers are requested to furnish their PAN to the Bank and those customers who are eligible to submit Form 121 are requested to submit Form No. 121 with their Permanent Account Number (PAN) along with the photocopy of the PAN Card immediately.
