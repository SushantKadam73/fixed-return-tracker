Super Senior Citizen Fixed Deposits (Akshay Nidhi)

A fixed deposit that offers optimal profits while being an uncomplicated investment option for really senior citizens. 80 years and above individuals are entitled to a preferential interest rate of 0.75% per year for all tenures. In addition to the higher interest rate, this fixed deposit account has several other advantages for the elderly. Super seniors can benefit from a constant and reliable income stream in their post-retirement years with the regular interest payment option. It makes a secure environment where money can grow steadily.
