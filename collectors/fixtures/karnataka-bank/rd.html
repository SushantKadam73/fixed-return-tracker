Advantages of recurring deposits

Recurring deposits (RDs) are perfect for individuals who aim to save regularly. They allow you to deposit a fixed amount every month, helping in building a savings habit. RDs offer interest rates similar to FDs, making them a lucrative savings instrument. They are ideal for short to medium-term financial goals, like funding education or planning a vacation.

Interest rates explained

The interest on recurring deposits is compounded quarterly and is usually similar to that of fixed deposits. The interest is calculated on the monthly balances, ensuring that each installment grows individually over the period of the deposit.

Important do’s and don’ts

Do maintain consistency in deposits to maximize benefits. Utilize the flexibility in choosing the tenure and deposit amount as per your financial capability. Don't miss any monthly deposit, as it may attract penalties and disrupt the compounding of interest.
