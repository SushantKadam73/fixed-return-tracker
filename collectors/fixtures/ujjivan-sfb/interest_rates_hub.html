      * Important Points: No Additional Interest Rate for Senior Citizens

      * Calculation Methodology applicable for Savings Accounts:

      1. Interest is calculated on day-end balances maintained in the savings account and paid out on a monthly basis
      2. The higher interest rate will be applied and paid only basis the balances maintained as per slabs.

      Ex: If a customer maintains ₹ 3,20,000/- in the savings account, 2.50% interest will be earned for ₹ 3,00,000/- and 3.00% interest will be earned for the remaining ₹ 20,000/-
