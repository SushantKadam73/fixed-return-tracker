Note:

  • Interest rates are subject to change from time to time. Deposit will be booked upon availability of clear funds with the bank at applicable interest rate as on the date of receipt of the funds by the bank.
  • The maximum aggregate amount that can be invested in the Tax Savings Fixed Deposit (u/s 80C of In-come Tax Act) under a single PAN is Rs. 150,000 during a financial year. The default tenure is 60 months (5 years) and it cannot be closed prematurely.
  • The revised rates are applicable for fresh Fixed Deposits and renewal of existing Fixed Deposits.
  • The Bank computes interest based on the actual number of days in a year. In case, the Deposit is spread over a leap and a non-leap year, the interest is calculated based on the number of days i.e.,366 days in a leap year & 365 days in a non-leap year.
  • No Interest would be payable if the depositor withdraws the Fixed Deposit amount within 7 days from the date of deposits(For Domestic & NRO Deposits).
  • For NRE Deposit - Minimun tenure will have to be 12 months (1 year).No interest will be paid if depositor withdraws it before completion of 12 months(1 year) from the date of deposit.
  • For Fixed Deposits with tenure up to 180 days, interest will be calculated at maturity as simple interest.
  • In Recurring Flexi Deposit, customers can do top up of minimum Rs 100 and up to 10 times of initial funding with maximum capping of Rs 100000.
  • The incentive for Senior citizens will be an additional spread of 0.25% over the above card rates w.e.f. 01st September 2026. This change will be applicable for new deposits and those getting renewed. Existing deposits shall continue to earn interest as per contracted rate till maturity. The benefit of senior citizens additional rate is not applicable on NRE and NRO deposits.
  • For Senior Citizen age of first applicant should be 60 years or above (HUF cannot avail Senior citizen benefits).
  • Please note that premature payment will attract 1% penalty on the interest earned during the period, for which the deposit has been kept with the bank and not at contracted rate.
  • Interest earned will be subject to Tax Deducted at Source as per Income Tax Laws
  • For interest rates of Rs.3 Crore and above, please contact the Branch
  • Outside Fund transfer including credit via IMPS/NEFT/Clearing cheques are not allowed in RFD account
  • Fixed Deposit(Without Premature facility), Tax-Saver Deposit and Recurring/Flexi Recurring Deposit are currently not available for NRE/NRO Deposit type
