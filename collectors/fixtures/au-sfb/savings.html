    How to Calculate Savings Account Interest Rate

    Savings account interest is calculated on your daily closing balance and is credited monthly to your account.

    Formula to Calculate Interest

    Interest = (Daily Closing Balance × Interest Rate × Number of Days) / 365

    Example Calculation

    Let’s say:

    • Daily Closing Balance = ₹1,00,000
    • Annual Interest Rate = 2.50%
    • Number of Days in Month = 30

    Interest = ₹1,00,000 × (2.50 / 100) × 30 / 365
    = ₹1,00,000 × 0.0250 × 30 / 365
    = ₹205

    Monthly Interest Credited

    In this case, ₹205 would be credited to your account monthly by AU Small Finance Bank, assuming a consistent daily balance of ₹1,00,000 and a 2.50% annual interest rate.

    Note:

    Actual interest earned may vary depending on:

    • Your daily balance fluctuations
    • The interest rate slabs applicable to your account
