RBI Retail Direct

One-stop solution for investment in Government Securities by Individual Investors

Safe... Simple... Direct!!!

A great avenue to diversify your investments absolutely free!!

RBI Retail Direct Scheme allows individual investors to invest directly in Government Securities such as Central Government Securities and State Government Securities through a digital mode.

The scheme provides the following facilities to retail investors:

  • Opening a securities account (Retail Direct Gilt Account) with RBI free of cost.
  • Buying Government Securities (G-Secs) in the primary auctions conducted by RBI.
  • Buying and selling G-Secs in the secondary market through NDS-OMli>

For more information, please visit:
https://rbiretaildirect.org.in/#/FaqRbiRetailDirect

You may also view the videos at:
https://rbiretaildirect.org.in/#/Tour

By clicking the Proceed/Continue button, you will be redirected to RBI Retail Direct, a platform operated by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI). The Bank is providing this access solely for customer convenience. Investments undertaken on the RBI Retail Direct platform are governed by RBI's terms, conditions, rules, and regulations. Customers may refer to RBI Retail Direct for all transaction-related services, information, and support.
