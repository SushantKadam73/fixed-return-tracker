    FAQs

    Can I opt for auto-renewal of my FD?-

    Yes! You can set your maturity instructions to auto-renew both your principal and interest amount or only the principal amount.

    What is the minimum deposit amount required?+

    The minimum deposit amount is ₹1,000.

    Can I book an FD online?+

    Yes, you can easily book an FD on the Equitas 2.0 Mobile Banking App or by logging into your Internet Banking account.

    Do senior citizens get additional benefits?+

    Yes. Resident Senior Citizens can enjoy an additional 0.5%* p.a interest on their FDs.

    Is my deposit insured?+

    Yes. All deposits are insured up to ₹5 lakh under the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) scheme.
